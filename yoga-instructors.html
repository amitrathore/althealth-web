No. You curate and share; the marketplace handles fulfillment and shipping.

Not unless you want it to. Most instructors put a QR code at the front of the room or in their bio — students discover when they're ready.

Yes — studios can run a house storefront, onboard their full teaching roster, and share revenue with instructors.

Yes. AltHealth is multi-brand by design — your existing partnerships continue alongside it.

Does this work for YTT programs?

Especially well. Curate a YTT-mandated kit, share it with every cohort, and earn on every enrollment's gear purchase.

Do I need a big following?

No. Your weekly class roster is enough to start earning.
