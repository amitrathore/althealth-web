Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this like Fullscript or Wellevate?

Similar idea, broader catalog. AltHealth isn't tied to a single distributor — you curate across practitioner-grade brands, functional foods, and tools, and you keep the client relationship.

Do I need to hold inventory?

No. You curate and share; the marketplace handles fulfillment, shipping, and reorders.

Is this MLM or affiliate marketing?

Neither. It's a curated marketplace with transparent, disclosed commissions — built for clinicians, not influencers.

Can my health coaches participate?

Yes — coaches, intake staff, and group-program facilitators can join as advocates and earn commissions too.

Does this conflict with my CDR/CNS scope of practice?

You're recommending, not dispensing or prescribing. Clients buy directly from the marketplace, and your disclosures are visible — designed to align with professional ethics codes.

Do I need a big audience?

No. Your existing client base is enough to start earning — and reorders compound over time.
