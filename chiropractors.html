The Solution

Your Virtual Storefront — on AltHealth or Your Own Domain.

  • Curate: Build product collections—mobility tools, recovery supplements, posture gear, sleep support—organized by goal and phase.
  • Share: Give every patient a simple link or QR code to your Virtual Storefront (in-office, follow-up texts, email).
  • Earn: When your recommendations lead to a purchase, you earn a commission. Full transparency on what's working.
  • Pro upgrade: Run your Virtual Storefront on your own domain or website — your brand, your audience, fully powered by AltHealth.

Empower Your Team: Onboard your front-desk or rehab staff as advocates. They help patients stay on-track with your recommendations, and earn commissions too.
