Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to hold inventory?

No. You curate and share; the marketplace handles fulfillment, shipping, and reorders.

I already have a supplement sponsor. Can I still join?

Yes. AltHealth is multi-brand by design — your existing partnerships continue alongside it, and you can add the gear, wearables, and recovery tools they don't sell.

Can my gym or studio participate?

Yes — gyms can run a house storefront, onboard their full coaching staff, and share revenue.

What about online coaches and remote programming?

Perfect fit. Drop your storefront link in the program PDF, the training app, or the welcome email. Reorders compound.

Is this affiliate marketing?

It's a curated marketplace with transparent commissions — closer to a pro dispensary than a Linktree of promo codes.

Do I need a big following?

No. Your roster of clients is enough to start earning.
